Abstract

We prove Mike Sheppard's conjecture in OEIS A381423, Comments, revision 8, that the exponent of the largest signed coefficient of the physicists' Hermite polynomial has successive differences in {1,-3}. A coefficient-ratio calculation gives an explicit formula and proves uniqueness, square-root growth, and the two asserted difference-block lengths. We give exact quadratic indices for the longer blocks. The corresponding absolute-coefficient calculation proves the conjecture in A381524 and the three formulas for equal absolute maxima in A381737. It also gives the absolute mode explicitly, locates its two difference-block types, and determines the asymptotic ratios of successive maximal coefficients.
